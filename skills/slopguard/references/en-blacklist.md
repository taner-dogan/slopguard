# English blacklist

Do not introduce these as new filler. Replace with the concrete thing, or delete the sentence. A word already in the codebase or used in a technical sense (`primary key`, `landscape` as photo format, `ecosystem` in biology) may stay.

## Verbs and hype

delve, harness, unlock, unleash, leverage (as verb), empower, elevate, foster, spearhead, showcase, boast, underscore, highlight (as verb for “emphasize”), emphasize, garner, accentuate, enhance, streamline, revolutionize, supercharge, reimagine, redefine, disrupt, democratize, synergize, captivate, resonate, navigate (metaphorical), optimize (as marketing speak)

## Noun fog

tapestry, landscape (abstract), realm, paradigm, ecosystem (except biology and real platform dependency graphs), interplay, synergy, testament, beacon, journey (for a product), game-changer, breakthrough (as marketing)

## Adjectives without measurement

cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, leading-edge, next-gen, world-class, best-in-class, enterprise-grade, battle-tested, blazingly, seamless, frictionless, robust, holistic, comprehensive, crucial, pivotal, paramount, vital, key (as praise adjective), intricate, meticulous, vibrant, groundbreaking, innovative, transformative, unprecedented, unparalleled, scalable (as praise), mission-critical, visionary, disruptive, immersive, intuitive, proactive, insightful, data-driven, future-proof, turnkey, plug-and-play, nuanced, multifaceted, commendable, enduring, invaluable

## Stiff verbs

utilize, commence, facilitate, necessitate, elucidate, ascertain, procure, endeavor, ameliorate, expedite, disseminate, pertaining to, in order to (use: to)

## Quantity fog

plethora, myriad, a variety of, a wide range of, numerous (when the number is knowable)

## Sentence-start transitions

Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, Firstly, Secondly, Finally, In conclusion, In summary, In a nutshell, That being said, At the end of the day, Moving forward, It's worth noting, It's important to note, First and foremost, Notably, Importantly, Interestingly, Basically, Essentially, Obviously, Clearly, In essence, As previously mentioned, Last but not least, Needless to say, To put it simply

## Office jargon

deep dive, circle back, touch base, pain point, move the needle, low-hanging fruit, net-net, folks, unpack (as talk, not literal unpacking)

## Phrases to delete

- In today's fast-paced world / digital age / landscape
- In the era of / In the world of / In the realm of
- Let's dive in / explore / unpack / break this down
- At its core / The reality is / Make no mistake / It's no secret
- When it comes to / In terms of / In the context of
- Not just X / It's not just about
- Plays a crucial role / stands as a testament / serves as a reminder
- Setting the stage / marking a shift / indelible mark / deeply rooted
- Rich cultural heritage / enduring legacy (without a concrete source)
- Designed to / built to help you / whether you're a X or a Y
- Say goodbye to / Meet the future of / Introducing the next
- Unlock the potential / take it to the next level / empower your workflow
- Actionable insights / best practices / thought leadership / key takeaways
- I hope this helps / Let me know if / Would you like me to

## Template UI lines

- Empower / Transform / Unleash / Supercharge your …
- Build faster. Scale smarter.
- The all-in-one platform for …
- Everything you need to …
- Trusted by teams at …
- Join thousands of …
- From idea to production in minutes
- Beautiful by default. Powerful under the hood.

If a sentence still fits another company after you swap the product name, rewrite it with the concrete function.
